⚡ How PavePulse Revolutionizes Road Maintenance

🎯

100m Spatial Clustering

When multiple citizens report the same pothole, our Haversine engine merges duplicate entries within 100 meters, boosting the recurrence score without fragmenting authority attention.

🧠

100-Point Risk Engine

Hazards are algorithmically graded based on Severity (40 pts) + Location Traffic (20 pts) + Safety Threat (25 pts) + Citizen Recurrence (15 pts) for objective triage.

📸

Photographic Evidence

Zero fake closures. Field officers must photograph the completed asphalt patch and submit remarks, creating a permanent audit trail before a ticket is resolved.

☁️

Cloud-Native on AWS

Architected for Amazon EC2 and S3 with automated Nginx reverse proxying and IAM role authentication — highly scalable yet costing $0.00 on AWS Free Tier.
